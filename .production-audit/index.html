What is VerityAI and who developed it?

VerityAI is a configurable AI assistant and business automation platform developed by VerityCore Consultancy (Pvt) Ltd in Zimbabwe. It can be tailored around your knowledge, data collection, customer conversations and approved workflows.

Can VerityAI be customised for my business?

Yes. VerityAI can be configured around your industry, fields, questions, knowledge, user permissions and approved actions. Eligible implementations can add integrations and automations for the systems your business uses.

Can VerityAI manage leads coming from Facebook or Instagram?

Yes. Send people from your campaign to your website assistant or business WhatsApp channel. VerityAI can answer questions, understand what they need and save their details for your team. Direct import from Facebook lead forms is not available yet.

Will the assistant know enough about my business?

You decide what it knows. Add your approved information, private files and public website pages, then choose the questions and welcome experience that suit your sales process.

Can it replace my sales team?

VerityAI is designed to make your team faster, not remove human judgment. It handles first responses, routine questions and qualification while your people focus on advice, negotiation and closing.

What happens when my AI credits run out?

AI responses pause when your available credits run out, so you never receive an unexpected usage bill. Paid customers can upgrade or add more credits.

How do I add VerityAI to my website?

Copy the secure installation script from your workspace into your website. Most website owners or developers can complete the setup in a few minutes.

Does WhatsApp AI work immediately?

The WhatsApp contact button is available on Launch. Automated WhatsApp replies are in Early Access on eligible plans and require an approved WhatsApp Business number.

Can I use VerityAI without VerityPack?

Yes. VerityAI is a standalone service with its own customers, products, opportunities, appointments and quotations. You do not need VerityPack to use it.

Can I add VerityAI to my current system or software?

Yes. You can add the website assistant with a secure installation script. Eligible plans can also connect VerityAI to supported business systems, with setup tailored to the software you already use.

How are payments handled?

Checkout is initiated securely through Paynow. Available card, mobile money and local payment methods depend on the merchant configuration shown during checkout. VerityAI does not store your payment card details.
